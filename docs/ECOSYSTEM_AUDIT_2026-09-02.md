# Bitey Ecosystem Audit — 2026-09-02

**Date:** 2026-09-02
**Scope:** Bitey IA Web as the general intelligence web channel.

## Assessment

Bitey Web is appropriately treated as a channel to the general Bitey IA intelligence layer rather than as a separate brain. Specialized products such as BiteFixes, JobIA and SBT retain their own domain authority and communicate through explicit contracts.

**Assessment:** 8.5/10 architectural maturity at this review point.

## Priority actions

1. Keep the general intelligence/channel boundary explicit.
2. Maintain independent UX and product identity for specialized products.
3. Enforce authorization and privacy boundaries when integrating specialized backends.
4. Prefer versioned APIs/contracts over direct cross-repository coupling.
5. Record dated integration evidence for major releases.
